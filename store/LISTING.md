## Description
Wind, tide times, waves and weather on one watch app, for surfing,
paddleboarding and canals around the UK.

- NOW: wind arrow, speed and gusts, waves, next high or low tide and how long until it
- TIDE: today's tide curve plus the next highs and lows
- NEXT 12H: hourly wind, gusts and wave heights
- WEATHER: temperature, rain chance, sea temperature, sunrise and sunset, swell
- Glance: wind, next tide and waves at a glance

Pick a place with GPS, from 56 UK surf and SUP spots, from your favourites, or
on the map. The map works with buttons only, so wet hands and touch-off are fine.

Away from the sea (canals, lakes), it switches to wind and weather only.

No account or sign-up. Data comes from Open-Meteo through your phone.
Tide times are modelled from forecast sea level. They are usually within about
10 minutes, and up to about 30 minutes out at double-high-water places such
as the Solent. For exact port times, add a free UKHO Admiralty API key in the app settings.

Not for navigation. Always check official sources before going out.

Open source (MIT): https://github.com/edleeman17/tideline
