Wind, tides and waves on your wrist. Built for SUP and surf.

## Description
Is it on? Check your wrist, not five apps.

Tideline puts wind, tide and waves on one screen, so you can decide in
seconds whether to paddle out, which way to launch, and when to come back in.
Made for stand-up paddleboarding, surfing and canal paddling
around the UK.

ONE LOOK BEFORE YOU GO
• Wind speed, gusts and a big direction arrow
• Wave height and period
• Next high or low tide and how long until it turns

PLAN YOUR SESSION
• 12-hour forecast: wind, gusts and waves hour by hour
• Today's tide curve with a "now" line and the next four tides
• Sea temperature, swell, rain chance, sunrise and sunset

ANY SPOT
• Your GPS position, 56 built-in UK surf and SUP spots, or your favourites
• Pick on map: move the target, press START (works with buttons only, so
  wet hands and touch lock are fine)
• Canals, rivers and lakes: inland spots switch to wind and weather only

ON YOUR WATCH FACE
• Glance shows wind, next tide and waves without opening the app
• Wind in mph, knots or km/h (set in the Connect IQ app)

NO ACCOUNT, NO SUBSCRIPTION
Free and open source. Data comes from Open-Meteo through your phone.
Tide times are modelled from forecast sea level and are usually within
about 10 minutes (up to 30 at double-tide places like the Solent).
Want exact port times? Add a free UKHO Admiralty API key in the settings.

Not for navigation. Always check official sources and conditions before
going out.

Source: https://github.com/edleeman17/tideline
